What's in each plan

Basic covers schedules, rotations, escalations and alerts by phone, text and push.

Pro adds follow-the-sun rotations, postmortem templates, an audit trail and priority support.

Add-ons

Single sign-on (SAML) is available on Pro as an add-on for $4 per seat per month, billed the same way as the plan.

Extra phone alerts in regions outside North America and Europe are billed at cost.

Questions

Agents in your support team can see who is on call without a paid seat.
