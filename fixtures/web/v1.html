Questions

Does every user need a paid seat?

Only people who go on call. Stakeholders who just read status updates are free on every plan.

What does the AI assistant do?

Our AI assistant drafts the incident summary while you work the problem, and you can switch it off for any team.

Can we change plans later?

Yes. Upgrades take effect right away, and we prorate the difference.

Prices are in US dollars and exclude taxes. Yearly plans are billed up front for the full year.
